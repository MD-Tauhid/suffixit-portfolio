How quickly can you provide candidates?
For common stacks and clear role briefs, we typically share shortlisted profiles within a few business days. First contributors can start in as little as 48 hours after a mutual fit is confirmed, depending on security and onboarding steps on your side.
What is your pricing model?
Most engagements use a simple monthly rate per full-time equivalent, with optional milestone pricing for fixed-scope work. Estimates include assumed working hours, overlap with your time zone, and seniority mix—so finance teams can forecast burn without hidden pass-throughs.
Can engineers overlap with our working hours?
Yes. We staff with your collaboration window in mind—daily standups, reviews, and incident coverage—and document expected overlap up front.
What happens if a match is not right?
We run structured check-ins in the first weeks. If fit is off, we replace the contributor at no additional finder fee for the transition window defined in your agreement.
How do you handle IP, access, and security?
Contributors work under your policies: corporate devices or VDI where required, least-privilege access, and NDAs aligned with your legal templates. We support SOC2-style expectations when your security team needs evidence packs.
Do you offer a trial period?
Many clients start with a short paid trial or a reduced squad for the first month. Terms are spelled out before kickoff so both sides can validate pace, communication, and craft.
