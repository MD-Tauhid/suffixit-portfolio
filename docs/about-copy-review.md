# About page copy review and blue banner

Reviewed 9 November 2026. The company voice is clear, practical and business-focused. The primary message is that Suffix IT combines ready-made software products, custom application development and wider ICT services for businesses in Bangladesh and beyond.

## Copy changes

- The hero now identifies the company, location and 2017 starting year, rather than relying only on an abstract slogan.
- The story names ERP, HR/payroll, field operations, custom applications, web/e-commerce, cloud, infrastructure and industrial IoT.
- Values and strengths describe discovery, scope, design/development/testing, learning, maintenance and client relationships.
- Mission and vision preserve the original intent, including client success, employee opportunities and social contribution.
- People cards remain practice areas, not a fabricated staff directory; placeholder photography stays identified.
- Recognition copy describes the published credentials without adding awards, expiry dates or independently verified certification claims.
- Careers copy describes relevant work and links to the source career page without inventing current vacancies or benefits.
- The final enquiry section uses layered curved bands in brand-blue shades. It is a responsive vector background with white text and a small conceptual expertise panel. No downloaded image or new animation dependency is required.
